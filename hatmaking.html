✅ Second Workshop: Another Great Success!

March 28th · Fusion on First

What a wonderful turnout for our final hat-making workshop before the Parade! Participants crafted incredible creations and left ready to march in style on April 5th. Thank you to everyone who joined us!

✅ First Workshop: A Great Success!

February 23rd · ASU FIDM · Fusion on First

What a great experience! We owe Dennita Sewell and Maria Behnen a tremendous amount of gratitude for opening up the incredible Fusion on First workshop to us. Everyone was friendly and encouraging.

Amina Hood, the visiting delightful milliner, couldn't have been more engaging and energetic. It was a pleasure to have FIDM's students join us and to share in Ms. Hood's Hat-Making Lecture. We are on a roll!
